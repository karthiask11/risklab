Privacy

Without signing in nothing about you leaves your device. Your holdings are kept only in your browser (local storage).

If you sign in, we store your email address, your name from Google (if you use Google) and the portfolios, plans and reports you save. They are stored in Google Firebase so you can open them on any device. Only you and the site's admin can read them; database rules block everyone else.

Activity log: while you are signed in, the site records when you sign in, which tools you open, the stocks in your saved portfolios and when you save something, so the admin can run and improve the site.

We use this data only to run the site and show you your work. We do not sell or share it, show ads, or use tracking or analytics cookies.

Your choices: rename or delete any portfolio, or delete your whole account and all saved portfolios with “Delete account” at the top of the page after signing in.

Questions or requests: open an issue at github.com/karthiask11/risklab. This notice follows the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.
